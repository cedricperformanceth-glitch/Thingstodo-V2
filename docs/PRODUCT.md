# Product

Things To Do Atlas is an independent editorial travel guide. Countries, cities, places and Things To Do are data; pages are reusable presentations of that data.

Adding a city must be predominantly content, media and configuration work. It must not require a city-specific component, route, client script or engine.

Practical addresses are `Place` records. Activities, attractions and landmarks are one `ThingToDo` family; a landmark is flagged with `isLandmark`, never duplicated. Editorial selection belongs to Atlas, while Favorites and My Trip belong to the visitor.
